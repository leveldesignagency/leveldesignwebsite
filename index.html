Process

How we work.

From discovery to launch and beyond.

  1. 01

    Discovery with internal stakeholders

    We map objectives, constraints, and success metrics with the people who own the outcome. Clarity creates speed.

  2. 02

    Information architecture & accessibility planning

    Structure and navigation that serve large content sets and diverse audiences. WCAG-aware from the start.

  3. 03

    Design with review checkpoints

    Prototypes and visual systems with clear sign-off points. Stakeholder-friendly, not chaos.

  4. 04

    Development & content migration

    Build, migrate, and hand over. Performant, maintainable, and ready for non-technical teams.

  5. 05

    Testing, compliance checks & launch

    Quality checks, accessibility review, and a controlled go-live. This won’t become chaos.

  6. 06

    Post-launch support

    Ongoing support, updates, and training so your site stays reliable and future-proof.
