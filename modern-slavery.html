Modern slavery statement

Last updated: 12 July 2026 · Financial year ending 31 March 2026

This statement is made pursuant to section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015. It sets out the steps LEVEL DESIGN AGENCY LTD has taken to prevent modern slavery and human trafficking in our business and supply chains.

Our business

LEVEL DESIGN AGENCY LTD is a UK-based design agency providing web design, branding, digital products, and related creative services. We work with clients across the United Kingdom and internationally.

Our commitment

We have zero tolerance for modern slavery, forced labour, human trafficking, or exploitation in any form. We expect the same standards from everyone we work with.

Our supply chains

Our supply chains are relatively short and primarily comprise professional services, software subscriptions, hosting providers, freelancers, and specialist contractors. We assess risk based on sector, geography, and engagement type.

Policies and due diligence

We aim to:

  • Work with reputable suppliers and partners
  • Include appropriate contractual expectations where relevant
  • Encourage reporting of concerns without retaliation
  • Review this statement annually and improve our approach as we grow

Training and awareness

Our team is expected to act with integrity and report any concerns. We will expand formal training as our operations and supplier base develop.

Approval

This statement was approved by the directors of LEVEL DESIGN AGENCY LTD on 12 July 2026.
