KLEEN.

A web app platform connecting domestic and commercial clients with specialist cleaners — end-to-end design and build from first wireframe to production.

Sector
Field services
Deliverables
Product design, platform build
Live
kleenapp.co.uk

The challenge

Cleaning marketplaces fail when the journey is vague — clients don’t know what they’re booking, and specialists can’t show their value. KLEEN needed clarity on both sides of the marketplace.

Our approach

We shaped a clearer user journey, stronger service presentation, and a cleaner path to conversion. Domestic and commercial flows stay distinct but share one design system — so the product feels unified without sacrificing specificity.

What it does

  • Domestic bookings

    Home clients browse services, compare options, and book with transparent pricing and scope.

  • Commercial accounts

    B2B flows for facilities teams — recurring schedules, invoicing-ready structure, and specialist matching.

  • Specialist network

    Cleaners present credentials and services clearly — building trust before the first job.
