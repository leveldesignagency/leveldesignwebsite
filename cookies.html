Cookies

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This page explains what cookies we use, why, and how you can control them. It sits alongside our privacy policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar tools (local storage, pixels) may be used for the same purposes. We use them to run the site and, with your permission, to understand how it is used.

Essential cookies

These are required for basic functions such as security, load balancing, and remembering your cookie choice. The site cannot work properly without them. They do not require consent.

Analytics cookies

If you choose Accept all on our banner, we may use analytics to see aggregate traffic (pages viewed, approximate location, device type). This helps us improve the site. Analytics stay off if you choose Essential only.

Your choices

  • Use the cookie banner when it appears
  • Clear site data in your browser to reset the choice
  • Block cookies in browser settings (some features may break)

We do not use cookies to sell your data or run third-party ad networks on this site.
